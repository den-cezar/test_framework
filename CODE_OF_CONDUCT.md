# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone who takes part in the project: in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and any other project space.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code, not about people.
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification before drawing conclusions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report incidents to the maintainer, [@den-cezar](https://github.com/den-cezar). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who repeatedly or seriously violate it. Enforcement follows the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
